About Us

MediCraft (Pty) Ltd is a 100% citizen-owned diagnostic solutions powerhouse headquartered in Gaborone, Botswana. Established and led by a US-trained Biomedical Engineer with over 20 years of experience at industry leaders Roche and Orthosurge, MediCraft is the authorized partner for Abbott Diagnostics in Botswana. We provide a full-service "Reagent Placement Model," delivering the world’s most advanced Alinity platforms backed by a local guarantee of technical sovereignty.

STRATEGIC IDENTITY

• Vision: To create a future where no Motswana waits for a life-critical result, realized through a self-reliant diagnostic network that sets the global benchmark for reliability and citizen-led engineering. • Mission: MediCraft: Precision Technology. Local Expertise. National Health. • Tagline: Diagnostics in Trusted Hands.

THE MEDICRAFT ADVANTAGE

• Technical Sovereignty: Unlike competitors who rely on foreign engineering support, MediCraft’s leadership and field teams are Gaborone-based, providing "first-call" resolution and 24-hour response times. • Citizen Empowerment: As a 100% citizen-owned entity, we prioritize local talent and align perfectly with Botswana’s 2026 Economic Diversification Drive (EDD) and procurement preferences. • Clinical Insight: Our leadership’s deep experience with legacy Roche systems allows us to identify and bridge gaps in current diagnostic workflows.
